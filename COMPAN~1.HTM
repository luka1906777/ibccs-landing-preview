Choosing a structure

Four common routes, and who each one suits

The right choice depends on what you sell, where your clients are and how you plan to take money out. That's the first thing we work through on the call.

Most common

Limited Liability Company (LLC)

Best for: companies with partners, staff or outside investors

  • Limits founders' personal liability
  • 15% corporate income tax on distributed profit
  • Base entity for IC and Virtual Zone status
Freelancers and consultants

Individual Entrepreneur (IE)

Best for: solo professionals and small operations

  • Available to residents and non-residents
  • Eligible IEs can apply for Small Business Status: 1% on turnover below 500,000 GEL a year
  • Simpler bookkeeping than an LLC
Software and maritime

International Company (IC) status

Best for: established IT service and maritime companies

  • Applied for by an existing Georgian LLC through the Revenue Service
  • 5% corporate income tax and 5% personal income tax on employee salaries
  • Dividends exempt from tax
  • Exemption from VAT and property tax
Export of software

IT Virtual Zone status

Best for: software developers serving clients outside Georgia

  • For software and digital product development for clients outside Georgia
  • 0% corporate income tax on profits from exported software services
  • No VAT on exported services
  • Obtained after the LLC is registered

Special statuses are granted by the competent state authorities on their own criteria. We assess your eligibility and prepare the application; approval is not guaranteed.

Questions

Before you book

Do I need to travel to Georgia?

Not for the company set-up itself. You can sign a power of attorney in your country, have it notarised and legalised, and our team acts on your behalf. We send you clear instructions for your jurisdiction.

Can a foreigner own 100% of a Georgian company?

Yes. There are no residency or nationality restrictions on shareholders or directors of a Georgian LLC.

Is there a minimum share capital?

No. Georgian law does not set a minimum share capital for an LLC.

Do you register the company yourselves?

No. Registration is carried out by the National Agency of Public Registry. We advise on the structure, prepare and review the documents, and represent you in the process.

Should I choose an LLC or an Individual Entrepreneur?

It depends on whether you work alone, how much you expect to earn and whether you plan to take on partners or investors. We compare both for your case on the discovery call.

Are you a government agency?

No. IBCCS TAX Georgia is an independent private advisory firm. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by NAPR, the Revenue Service or any other state authority of Georgia.
