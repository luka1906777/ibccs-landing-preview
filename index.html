Georgian tax basics

The rules your accountant works with every month

A short reference for founders new to Georgia. Whether a rule applies to you depends on your structure and activity, which is exactly what we check on the first call.

TopicGeneral rule
Corporate income taxGenerally 15%, charged when profits are distributed. Reinvested profit is not taxed under this model.
VAT registrationGenerally mandatory once taxable turnover exceeds 100,000 GEL within a continuous 12-month period. Voluntary registration is also possible.
Personal income taxGenerally 20% for individuals.
Small Business StatusAvailable to eligible individual entrepreneurs with annual turnover below 500,000 GEL, with a 1% rate on turnover.

General information only, not tax advice. Rates, thresholds and eligibility conditions change and depend on your circumstances. Last reviewed: October 2026.

Questions

Before you book

Can you take over from our current accountant?

Yes. During onboarding we review prior periods. If records need correcting or reconstructing, we tell you what we found and fix it as part of a restoration scope.

How is the price set?

After the discovery call we send a written scope and estimate. It depends mainly on monthly transaction volume, VAT status, number of employees and reporting requirements.

Do you prepare IFRS financial statements?

Yes. We prepare statements under IFRS where required and apply the simplified frameworks and exemptions available to qualifying small enterprises. We also handle SARAS reporting and annual submissions.

Which accounting software do you use?

We work with 1C, Apex, Zoho Books, Balansi and FMG, and can also help implement and manage accounting software if you don't have one yet.

Are you a government agency?

No. IBCCS TAX Georgia is an independent private advisory firm. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by the Revenue Service or any other state authority of Georgia.
